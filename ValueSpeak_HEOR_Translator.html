The evidence gap

Indian appraisals are weakest where decisions are hardest

A 2025 peer-reviewed analysis scored 33 HTAIn studies against the Indian Reference Case. Equity and subgroup analysis came last.

25.7%
adherence on equity, the lowest-scoring domain
24.3%
adherence on heterogeneity: 10 studies identified subgroups, only 6 analysed them
60.6%
of studies included a budget impact analysis
6 of 33
studies scored above 8 against the Indian Reference Case

Source: Mohan, Muraleedharan, Subramanian. Health Research Policy and Systems, 2025. The authors recommend subgroup and distributional analysis where data allow, and appraisal of operational feasibility for every intervention.

Two more gaps appear in the literature. Evidence reaches decision-makers as static reports that leave non-technical readers to work out what a result means for them. And a 2025 scoping review of Indian HTA names private-sector dominance, stakeholder resistance and uncertain funding as barriers to acting on recommendations.

What ValueSpeak tests. Whether a light layer that sits on top of any model can close these gaps: flag missing equity and implementation evidence, separate value from affordability, score how complete the evidence package is, and write for each reader. This is a literature-supported opportunity to investigate with buyers, not proof that other products lack these features.

Equity & Decision Brief

The Equity-to-Decision Bridge: who may benefit, whether the budget can carry it, whether the system can deliver it, how complete the evidence is, and a brief for each reader.

A

Equity and subgroup review

Compare costs and outcomes for groups you supply. ValueSpeak never infers group results from averages.

B

Affordability

Cost-effective and affordable within a specific budget are different questions. Budget impact follows the Indian guideline: payer perspective, 1 to 4 years, undiscounted.

C

Implementation readiness

Mark each item from your own knowledge or sources. Nothing is pre-filled.

D

Decision Readiness Matrix

A prototype heuristic that measures how complete the decision support is. It does not say whether a treatment is effective, safe, cost-effective or recommended.

E

Audience brief

Pick the reader. Hover a highlighted number to see the ledger line it came from.

F

Export and share

The report includes the decision question, perspective, population, comparator, sources, assumptions, uncertainty, equity findings, implementation checklist, missing evidence, limitations and the prototype disclaimer.
